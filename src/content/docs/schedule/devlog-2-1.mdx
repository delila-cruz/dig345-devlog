## Create an Astro Starlight DevLog site

Follow the Astro Starlight tutorial to create a JAMstack website with Astro.js. You will use this site to post DevLog entries to record your work throughout the semester.  :40 min

1. Fork an Astro Starlight site and update project structure.
2. Publish Astro Starlight using Github Actions ✏️ [https://delila-cruz.github.io/dig345-devlog/](https://delila-cruz.github.io/dig345-devlog/)
3. Add custom CSS to your theme
4. Import and use an Astro component
5. Continue customizing your site using the Starlight Docs and explain what you did. ✏️ I added colored asides to the bottom of this DevLog to make it easier to see my responses.
6. Add content into: devlog-1-3.mdx (the DevLog from the previous week)
7. Add this file and devlog-2-1.mdx for today, and add content.
8. Watch 🎬 Heydon Pickering Why is everything binary?. Post a reading response in the 2-1 DevLog per information in the Assignments.
9. Write a reflection explaining what you did learned in this DevLog. Link to sources.

<Aside type="tip" icon="seti:notebook" title="Reading Response">
  ##### Summary
  In this video, Pickering reflects on how we tend to see our society as black and white, as if there are only two options for everything instead of a spectrum of possibilities.
  ##### Quote
  *"But the idea that everyone belongs to one of two archetypes is seductive in its simplicity."*
  
  I just like how they use the word "seductive"" in this quote. I think it's the perfect way to describe the way we settle for easy answers---it's alluring.
  ##### Comment
  I thought this video was pretty interesting! I don't think I ever really questioned how deep our norm of binary thinking goes. Of course I've thought about it in terms of different social issues, but this video brought up examples of other ways we see things as binary.
  ##### Discussion Question
  Is there anything that could be just binary, or is it possible that everything is a spectrum? Could be fun to think about.
  ##### Related Work
  This kind of reminds me of the villain "The Spot" in the most recent Spiderverse movie. He's literally black and white, and he kind of succumbs to his all-or-nothing thinking after his accident and becomes a villain because of it.
</Aside>
